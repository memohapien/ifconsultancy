Who we are

A senior advisory firm at the intersection of business, technology and human systems.

Founded in 2015, IF Consultancy is a strategic transformation partner for organizations navigating complexity, growth, technology and change. We work where the hardest questions sit: where to compete, how to operate, what to build — and whether the organization is genuinely capable of delivering it.

We are not a training company and not a single-discipline firm. Our teams bring strategy consulting, organizational transformation, leadership development and emerging technology into one mandate, because in practice these challenges never arrive separately.

Senior by default
Every mandate is led by practitioners who have carried executive accountability themselves.
Evidence before opinion
Diagnostics, data and behavioral insight come first; recommendations follow.
Built to be delivered
We stay until the operating model, the capability and the culture actually move.
